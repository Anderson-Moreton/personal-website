About me

I am Anderson Moreton, a Full Stack Developer with a degree in Systems Analysis and Development from FMU – São Paulo. My main focus is building modern, scalable web applications using Angular, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, MySQL, and Docker. I enjoy transforming ideas into clean, responsive, and maintainable solutions while continuously improving my technical skills.

My professional journey combines academic education with hands-on experience in software development and data analysis. Throughout this path, I have developed projects involving RESTful APIs, JWT authentication, relational databases, responsive interfaces, and complete frontend-backend integration. Every project has been an opportunity to apply best practices, strengthen problem-solving skills, and deepen my understanding of modern web development.

After relocating to Portugal, I expanded my professional experience by working in both the IT retail and data analytics sectors. These experiences strengthened my analytical thinking, adaptability, and communication skills. Today, my primary goal is to continue growing as a Full Stack Developer, contributing to innovative projects while expanding my expertise in Angular and the modern JavaScript ecosystem.
